Methodology & Diagnostics Scope

Space2Plate evaluates optical imagery using an Excess Green Index (ExG) vegetation model coupled with spectral chlorosis and brown necrosis detection.

  • Chlorosis: Detects chlorophyll loss from nutrient deficiency or water stress.
  • Necrosis: Flags cellular death or foliar disease before canopy loss occurs.
  • Prescriptive Output: Formulates micro-dosing and environmental adjustments.
ℹ️ Prototype optical index simulation for hackathon demonstration. Not scientifically certified flight hardware.
